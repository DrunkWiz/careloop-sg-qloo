CareLoop SG · Powered by Qloo Taste AI

Quiet, culturally grounded support for seniors with high blood pressure, between doctor visits

The problem

Most of hypertension care happens at home, between visits. Readings drift, pills get missed, a senior feels unwell or isolated and says nothing. Alert apps fire fixed "BP over 140" alarms so often that families stop listening — while generic AI companions suggest generic salads, loud gyms, or unsafe foods that ignore a senior's cultural roots, medications, and the day's weather.

What CareLoop does

A short daily check-in, by voice or big buttons, in English, Singlish, Mandarin, Malay or Tamil. CareLoop learns each senior's own normal, stays quiet while things are normal, and steps up gently when patterns persist. Between check-ins, Qloo Taste AI turns a senior's cultural memories (e.g. Teresa Teng, Teochew porridge, 1960s Shaw Brothers films, P. Ramlee) into safe, nearby meals, nostalgic playlists, and Active Ageing Centre programmes — filtered through deterministic clinical, pharmacist-leaflet, and live Singapore haze rules.

Who uses it

  • Seniors: a large-text app with voice check-ins, plain-word medicine guides, Qloo-matched daily activities, and one-tap memory erasure.
  • Family: quiet trends, evidence-backed handovers, a Qloo Family Visit Kit (what to tap/bring on Sunday), and a one-page GP brief.
  • Centre staff: a caseload console sorted by urgency, plus a Multi-Senior Qloo Group Intersection & Kopi Kakis buddy matcher.

How Qloo Taste AI + Clinical Rules work together

  • 1. Resolve (qloo_search): maps cultural seeds to canonical Qloo entity IDs.
  • 2. Cross-Domain (qloo_insights): queries urn:entity:place, artist, movie with signal.demographics.age=55_and_older & Singapore WKT polygons.
  • 3. Clinical Filter (check_health_constraints): blocks high-sodium, gout purine, statin/amlodipine grapefruit-pomelo leaflet clashes, and outdoor walks when PSI > 100.
  • 4. Guardrail (guardrail_check): verifies bilingual wording in EN / ZH / MS / TA.

How it uses AI, safely

Rules decide; AI and Qloo handle language and cultural taste. A language model reads check-ins to catch what keyword rules miss ("my chest feel like got elephant sitting on top" → chest-pain red flag) and words gentle notes around Qloo's structured entity graph. Neither the LLM nor Qloo ever decides whether a reading is dangerous or overrides a medical/medication/haze filter. Every message passes guardrail.py (no diagnosis, no dose changes, no invented numbers) or falls back to a fixed template.
